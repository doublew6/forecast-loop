## Evidence check

This is synthetic example content. Record the source, observation time,
content hash, and evidence cutoff before drawing a conclusion. Unverified
material remains a lead, not a confirmed fact.

<!-- section:counter-evidence -->
## Counter-evidence

State an observable fact that would weaken the current view. Repeated copies of
the same report do not count as independent sources.
